About the platform

India's dedicated learning and career platform for geospatial technology

GEOSPATIAlS is an online learning and professional platform connecting learners, experts and industry, strongly focused on ACADEMIC UPSKILLING and ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITY BUILDING of the Geospatial Industry, encouraging Very Short-Term Training Programs for In-Demand Skills.

It covers the full discipline: GIS, Remote Sensing, Photogrammetry, LiDAR, Drone (UAV) Mapping, Surveying and GNSS, GeoAI and Spatial Data Science, Web GIS and Digital Twins.

Our vision

A trusted home for geospatial growth

To be the most trusted place where geospatial skills are built through practice and recognised by industry.

Our mission

Accessible, verifiable, career-connected

To make high-quality, practical geospatial education available to everyone, prove it with verifiable credentials, and connect it directly to jobs, internships and projects.
