About the platform

India's dedicated learning and career platform for geospatial technology

GEOSPATIALS is a purpose-built digital platform that brings learners, expert trainers and industry organisations together on one trusted network. It covers the full discipline: GIS, remote sensing, photogrammetry, LiDAR, drone (UAV) mapping, surveying and GNSS, GeoAI and spatial data science, web GIS and digital twins.

Location intelligence now shapes decisions in urban planning, agriculture, utilities, water management, land administration, infrastructure, forestry, environment and disaster response. Yet quality training, credible certification and genuine industry contact are often scattered across many places. GEOSPATIALS brings structured learning, hands-on practice, verified credentials and real opportunities into a single journey.

Our vision

A trusted home for geospatial growth

To be the most trusted place where geospatial skills are built through practice and recognised by industry.

Our mission

Accessible, verifiable, career-connected

To make high-quality, practical geospatial education available to everyone, prove it with verifiable credentials, and connect it directly to jobs, internships and projects.
